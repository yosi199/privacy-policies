Frequently Asked Questions

How do I mix multiple sounds?

Go to the Mixer tab, then tap on any category to expand it. Tap the sounds you want to add to your mix. You can combine up to 4 sounds and adjust each volume individually.

How does the sleep timer work?

Go to the Timer tab, select your desired duration, and tap Start Timer. Your sounds will gently fade out when the timer ends.

Will sounds play in the background?

Yes! Sounds continue playing when you lock your phone or switch to other apps.

How do I stop all sounds?

Tap the "Stop All" button in the Mixer tab, or use the stop button in the Now Playing section on the Sounds tab.
