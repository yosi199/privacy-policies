Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 28, 2026

Thank you for using MetadataRemover. We built this app with privacy as our core principle.

Our Promise: Your photos never leave your device. All metadata processing happens entirely on your iPhone. We cannot see, access, or store your photos.

Information We Collect

We do not collect any personal information or photo data.

MetadataRemover is designed to protect your privacy. The app:

  • Does not upload your photos to any server
  • Does not collect personal data
  • Does not track your location
  • Does not use analytics or tracking services
  • Does not share any data with third parties
  • Does not require an account or login

Photo Processing

All photo metadata reading and removal is performed locally on your device using Apple's native ImageIO framework. Your photos are processed in memory and are never transmitted over the internet.

Data Stored on Your Device

The app stores minimal data locally on your device:

  • Usage counter: Tracks daily free tier usage (resets daily)
  • Subscription status: Managed by Apple's StoreKit
  • Preferences: App settings and language preference

This data never leaves your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app.

Subscriptions

Premium subscriptions are processed entirely through Apple's App Store. We do not have access to your payment information. Subscription management is handled by Apple in your device Settings.

Third-Party Services

MetadataRemover does not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services. We use only Apple's native frameworks:

  • StoreKit: For in-app purchases (managed by Apple)
  • PhotoKit: For accessing photos you select (with your permission)
  • ImageIO: For reading and removing metadata locally

Photo Library Access

The app requests permission to access your Photo Library. This access is used solely to:

  • Read photos you select for metadata viewing/removal
  • Save cleaned photos back to your library (when you choose to save)

You can revoke this permission at any time in Settings > MetadataRemover.

Children's Privacy

Our app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. The app is rated 4+ and is safe for all ages.

Data Security

Since we don't collect or transmit your data, there is no risk of data breaches affecting your photos or personal information through our app.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
