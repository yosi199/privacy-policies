Frequently Asked Questions

What is photo metadata?

Metadata is hidden information embedded in your photos. It can include GPS coordinates (where the photo was taken), camera make and model, date and time, and technical settings like ISO and aperture. This data can reveal your location and habits when you share photos online.

How do I remove metadata from a photo?

Tap "Select Photo" on the home screen, choose a photo from your library, and you'll see what it reveals. Tap "Remove All Metadata" at the bottom. The cleaned copy can then be saved to your library or shared directly. On iPad and Mac you can also drag photos onto the app or open them from Files or Finder.

Does removing metadata affect photo quality?

No. MetadataRemover preserves the full image quality. Only the invisible metadata is stripped — your photo's pixels, resolution, and colors remain exactly the same.

Are my photos uploaded anywhere?

No. All processing happens entirely on your device. Your photos never leave your iPhone, iPad or Mac. We cannot see or access your photos in any way.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Reading and removing metadata needs no internet connection. Offline, the app can't show place names for a photo's location, load subscription options, or sign you in.

Which devices and languages are supported?

iPhone, iPad and Mac. The app is available in English and Hebrew.

What's included in the free version?

Free users can view the privacy report for any photo and remove metadata from 1 photo per day. The daily limit resets at midnight local time and counts per device.

What does Premium include?

Premium unlocks unlimited removal, up to 20 photos and videos at once, library clean-up, choosing what to remove, editing metadata, presets, and Shortcuts. You can choose a monthly or yearly subscription or a one-time Lifetime purchase.

How do I process multiple photos at once?

Premium users can tap "Select Photos" and choose up to 20 photos and videos. Review each one, select which to clean, and remove metadata from all selected items at once.

How do I restore my purchase?

On the Premium screen, tap "Restore Purchases". Make sure you're signed in to the same Apple Account used for the original purchase. Purchases made on iPhone also unlock iPad and Mac with the same Apple Account.

Do I need an account?

No. The app works fully without an account. Signing in with Apple or Google is optional and has one purpose: Premium follows you to your other devices without Restore Purchases, including devices on a different Apple Account.

Where: on iPhone, Home > "Sync Premium Across Devices"; on iPad and Mac, Settings > Account. Signing out never removes a purchase made on that device.

How do I delete my account and my data?

In the app: open the account screen (iPhone: the account row on Home; iPad and Mac: Settings > Account) and choose "Delete Account". Your account (account ID, e-mail and name) is deleted. If you used Sign in with Apple, the app's permission is revoked as well.

Purchase record: the purchase history kept by our purchase service (RevenueCat) for your account is not deleted automatically. To have it deleted too, or if you can't open the app, e-mail com.loop.to.infinity@gmail.com from the address of your account and we delete it and confirm.

Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription or refund a purchase; your purchase stays restorable on your Apple Account. Your photos were never stored by us, so there is nothing to delete there. Details are in the Privacy Policy.

How do I turn off usage data and crash reports?

iPhone and iPad: Settings > Apps > MetadataRemover > Share Anonymous Usage Data. Mac: MetadataRemover > Settings > Privacy. They never include your photos or their metadata.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed through Apple. On iPhone and iPad go to Settings > [Your Name] > Subscriptions; on the Mac, App Store > Account. Find MetadataRemover and cancel. You keep access until the end of the billing period.

Why can't I see GPS location on the map?

Not all photos contain GPS data. Screenshots, photos with location services disabled, and images downloaded from the web typically don't have GPS metadata. The map preview only appears when GPS coordinates are present.

The app says "No Risk Found" — is my photo already clean?

Yes. The photo doesn't reveal personal information. This commonly happens with screenshots, images received through messaging apps, or photos that have already been cleaned.

How do I report a problem?

E-mail com.loop.to.infinity@gmail.com with your device, system version, the app version, and what happened. Please don't send photos that contain private information.

Tip: For maximum privacy, clean your photos before sharing on social media, dating apps, online marketplaces, or anywhere public. GPS metadata can reveal your home address, workplace, and daily routines.
